ABOUT THE PROJECT

AI for a More Sustainable Campus

Campus Energy AI compares real-time consumption data against expected usage patterns for every building on campus. When actual demand drifts from what the model expects, the system flags it, explains a likely cause, and suggests a concrete next step — turning raw meter data into decisions campus facilities teams can act on the same day.

01Monitor

Track energy usage across every campus building, continuously.

02Detect

Catch abnormal consumption as it happens, not at month-end.

03Act

Get plain-language, AI-generated recommendations your team can use.
